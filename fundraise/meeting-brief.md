## The 60-second pitch
Bitcoiners are asset-rich and cash-poor by choice. Selling is a taxable event and gives up the upside, so they will not sell, but they still need dollars. The people who tried to solve this (Celsius, BlockFi) blew up rehypothecating. Iron is the version that structurally cannot: 2-of-3 multisig, the user holds a key. Deposit bitcoin, get a USDT credit line, spend on a Visa card or over Lightning, repay on your terms. Overcollateralized, so a fraction of Chase's charge-offs. We make money on the net interest spread, interchange, and thin FX, never by lending out the collateral.

## The numbers he will drill (have them cold)
- **LTV / liquidation:** 40 to 50% to start (up to 60% top grade), liquidation near 85% on a public independent oracle, warned at every band.
- **Custody / bridge:** native BTC in 2-of-3 multisig (never wrapped); it secures a USDT facility, it is never sold to fund the line.
- **Unit economics:** $100k MRR = $1.2M ARR = loan book x (net spread ~4% + interchange/FX ~3.6%). Hit at ~6,000 cardholders (~$48M locked), a beachhead number, not a moonshot.
- **The ask:** $750k pre-seed equity (team, PH licensing, launch, first cardholders). The loan book is funded by a SEPARATE USDT debt facility, so it does not dilute.

## Objections, pre-loaded
- **"Stablecoin exposure?"** The borrowed unit is dollars (what users want), but the collateral and the moat are pure native bitcoin. Passes our own stablecoin test and your "bitcoin's properties are the point" filter.
- **"Why not Ledn / Coinbase?"** They lend. They do not put a spendable card and local rails in a user's pocket in Manila, and they have no multi-issuer redundancy. The card + emerging-market spend layer is the wedge.
- **"Regulatory?"** Licensed rails from day one: a Visa principal-member issuer with multi-issuer redundancy, BSP/EMI for PH, MAS track for the holdco. Licensing is the right to exist, not a "later" item.
- **"Partners signed?"** No, and we will never claim one that is not. IBEX is the intended Lightning rail, in conversation, not signed.

## Close
"Everyone else sells a bitcoin loan. We are building the bitcoin bank, the most trustworthy one, for the people who refuse to sell. I want you in the first round."
