## Rules
- **One CTA, every time:** iron.credit. Never bury it.
- **Show, do not tell:** the site and the card are the proof (Elon: prototypes are proof).
- **Concede the weak point:** on the "savings app" days, admit stablecoins won that fight. It buys credibility for the credit thesis.
- **Never name an unsigned partner.** Same rule as the pitch.
- **Measure:** signups per post on Vercel. Kill the themes that do not move it, double the ones that do.
