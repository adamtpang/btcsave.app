## The model (the Lindy credit business)

`Net interest margin = loan yield − cost of funds − credit losses − opex`

- Loan yield: 6 to 16% by grade (~10% typical).
- Cost of funds: the facility rate. This is the whole business; negotiate hard.
- Credit losses: near zero by overcollateralization (40 to 50% LTV, ~85% liquidation).
- $100k MRR = $1.2M ARR, reached at roughly 6,000 cardholders (bitcoiner mix, ~$48M locked).

## The liability-side rules (how lenders actually die)

1. **Never borrow BTC to lend dollars.** Currency mismatch is a Celsius-grade hole. Facility must land in dollars.
2. **Committed multi-year term, never callable.** Duration mismatch killed the S&Ls and SVB.
3. **No funder above ~40% of liabilities.** Concentration on the liability side is the same rule as on the collateral side.
4. **Gap risk is the real charge-off.** Stress test a 40% overnight drop.
5. **Match the rate basis.** Fixed-to-fixed, or re-price borrower APR with funding cost.
6. **Keep a first-loss equity cushion and a liquidity buffer.** Never be forced to sell.
7. **Covenants tied to book performance, not BTC price.**
