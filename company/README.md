# Iron: the company

Iron (IRO on Summon) is a non-custodial bitcoin-backed credit card. Lock bitcoin as collateral (2-of-3 multisig, the user holds a key), borrow dollars, spend on a Visa card, never sell.

- **Operating mode:** manual. Routing suggests, the board (Adam) dispatches. Nothing executes unpointed.
- **Org:** 9 seats. Cofounder plus the core 8 (engineering, design, marketing, sales, finance, operations, support, legal). Product is not a ninth department: the Cofounder owns product strategy, Engineering owns product execution.
- **Model ladder:** Cofounder on claude-fable-5, the core 8 on opus, subagents on grok when they exist.
- **Roadmap stage:** 2 of 8, "Found it". See the constraint below.

## The constraint (stage 2 exit, unproven)
The roadmap exit for stage 2 is "one real conversation or preorder from a stranger, written down." Iron has a site, a deck, unit economics, an investor pipeline, and a licensing plan, but **no logged conversation with an actual borrower.** Everything downstream (the raise, licensing, launch) is being built on unproven demand. Until a real customer conversation is written down, that is the binding constraint.
